Mark options

Chosen: G7, at the end of this page. Four directions side by side, each at full size, as an app icon down to 16 px, and in the tray (idle: hollow block, busy: yellow block). The colour rule is the same for all: ink or paper for the shape, yellow only for the block that moves.

Making it a G

The first refinements read as a C: the opening was too big and the bar sat above the end of the ring. A G needs a small opening and a bar at mid height that runs in from the right. The rounded-square G, refined: a taller mouth and the yellow block joined to the black spur.
